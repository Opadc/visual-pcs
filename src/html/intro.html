Ethernet PCS, for people who already know SerDes

Following a PCS lane from MAC to MAC across a 200G link

A 200GBASE-R PCS splits one stream into 8 logical lanes, and the PMA squeezes those onto however many SerDes lanes the interface has. The receiver gets them back in whatever order and timing the wires deliver, and the alignment markers in the data put them back together. The lab below runs that whole path. Pick a lane, change the width, then break something.

PCS lane
A logical stream the PCS creates and tracks. It is identified by the lane number in its alignment markers. 200GBASE-R has 8.
PMA lane
A lane at the PCS-to-PMA service interface. On transmit, PMA lane i carries PCS lane i. On receive, any PCS lane can arrive on any PMA lane.
SerDes lane
A physical electrical or optical lane: 8, 4 or 2 of them at 200G. Each one may bit-multiplex several PCS lanes.
